About The Tier List

Brawl Stars has 108 Brawlers in the game. Plus, it can be difficult to unlock them when you first start playing. So, we've compiled a tier list of all the Brawlers to help you choose what characters you should unlock or play as during the current meta.

The list above is calculated with recent battle log data from the top 200 players in the world for all game modes. You can view each Brawler's win rate, pick rate, and meta score. The meta score is a certain combination of the win and pick rates to better compare each character.

This means you can come back anytime to get the most up-to-date stats for the best Brawlers. Even while maps, game modes, seasons, and patches are always changing things up. You can also view previous days by using the History tab or see the combined list of all 30 days with the General tab.

The Tier list is structured in the following categories:

S - Top-tier. Generally considered the best and most common choices.

A - Great. Worthwhile in their own right, but especially as a compliment to more powerful Brawlers.

B - Good. You won't be unhappy choosing these Brawlers, and you might even find they synergize well with others.

C - Okay. Typically, average Brawlers whose inclusion begins to limit your skill ceiling.

D - Bad. Situational at best.

F - Bottom-tier. Generally considered the worst and least optimal to play.
